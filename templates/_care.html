Le service

You are staying in a house, not checking into one

Arriving

Someone comes out to the car. Usually Bruce first — he is a French bulldog and considers the gates his — and often Karina behind him. There is no desk to stand at and nothing to sign. Your bags go up while you are still looking at the valley.

The beds

Hand-embroidered vintage linen, most of it found and collected rather than ordered, so no two rooms are dressed the same. The beds themselves are new and very good. It is the one place in this house where nothing has been compromised.

Breakfast

Continental, though not the French idea of it — nobody is handing you a croissant and pointing at the coffee. It is laid properly and it is enough to walk the valley on.

The walls

Unrestored, and staying that way while conservators work along them. Original eighteenth-century plaster with the overpaint lifted by hand. You can sleep in a finished room in a great many houses. There are very few where the room around you is being uncovered.

Bruce lives here, along with the cats and the horses. If anyone in your party has an allergy, tell us when you book and we will arrange the house around it.
