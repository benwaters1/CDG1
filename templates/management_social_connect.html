Getting the details

  1. An Instagram Business account, linked to a Facebook Page.
  2. A Meta app at developers.facebook.com, of type Business. Creating it is a form — nobody approves it. Its App ID and App secret are under App settings → Basic.
  3. In the Graph API Explorer, pick the app, choose Get User Access Token, and tick {% for p in state.permissions %}{{ p }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. If the Page is held in a business portfolio, tick {% for p in state.portfolio_permissions %}{{ p }}{{ " and " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} as well. Generate it and paste it above.
  4. That token lasts about an hour. With the app ID and secret in, saving it swaps it for the Page's own token, which Meta reports no expiry for, and the app asks Meta about it every day after.
  5. About every ninety days Meta stops Instagram's permissions until the person who made the token authorises the app again. The owner home says so two weeks ahead, by Meta's own date, and the answer is step 3 again.

App Review is not needed. It grants Advanced access, which is for letting other people connect their own accounts. The house posts to its own, and Standard access covers that automatically. If you are ever asked to verify the business, you have strayed into requesting Advanced — back out of it.
