Straight With You

This Is Not For Everyone

We would rather you knew now than found out on the first evening.

Not if you want a hotel
There is no reception desk, no lift, no turndown and no room service. Someone will meet you and then leave you alone
Not if the walls will bother you
Most of the house is bare plaster and weathered stone, and it is illegal for us to change it. If that reads as unfinished rather than as the point, this is the wrong house
Not for a party
Sound carries through a stone building and the valley is quiet at night. Weddings and events are taken separately, one at a time
Not for very young children
Eight and over. Original staircases, unrestored rooms and a working restoration next door
Not if you need to be online
Starlink reaches some rooms and not others. Electricity is deliberately limited and the evenings are lit by candle

If none of that puts you off, you are exactly who this is for.

Worth Bringing

What to Pack

Everything you need is here. These are the things guests wish they had brought.

A layer more than you think
Stone walls a metre thick hold the cold as well as the heat. Warm in August, genuinely cold in the unrestored rooms in winter
Shoes for the valley
The parkland, the drive and anything at Montségur or Beille. Not for the house — the floors are new
A torch, if you wake in the night
Electricity is limited by design. There is light where you need it and not much beyond that
Whatever you take for an allergy
Six cats, two dogs and an old house with open doors
Cash for the markets
Most stalls at Les Cabannes and Mirepoix do not take cards
Not a hairdryer or an adaptor
Both are in the rooms. European sockets, and towels and linen are ours

En arrivantOn arriving

No Reception Desk

What Happens When You Pull Up

  1. 1

    You come through the gates and up the drive

    A quarter of a mile of it, and the house appears at the top. Park anywhere on the gravel — there is no allocated space and no charge.

  2. 2

    Someone is expecting you

    Not at a desk. We will have your arrival time from the booking, and whoever is on that day will come out to the steps. If nobody appears within a minute or two, the number on your confirmation rings a phone in the kitchen.

  3. 3

    Your bags go up, and so do you

    There is no lift and the original staircase is the only way to the first floor. Tell us in advance if that is a problem and we will put you on the ground floor.

  4. 4

    Then you are left alone

    Tea, coffee and the salons are yours from the moment you arrive. Dinner, if it is running that night, is at {{ settings['dinner_time'] if settings and settings['dinner_time'] else 'eight' }}. Nobody will knock.

Arriving late Tell us and it is fine — the drive is unlit and the gates are the only thing you need to find. If you have not arrived and we have not heard, someone will ring you.
