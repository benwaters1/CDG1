Une maison de famille A family house

The Story

A Family House

When Karina Waters bought Château de Gudanes, it had stood empty for generations, and much of it could not be entered. Since then the restoration has been followed the world over, room by room, and the house has begun, slowly, to live again. In 2016 the restoration received the Medal of Honour of the Fondation Prince Louis de Polignac, presented by Prince Albert II of Monaco.

Gudanes is a family house, not a hotel. Guests are welcomed into it as it returns, and the workshops, the full Gudanes experience, let them stay long enough to become part of its story.

La famille The family

The Family

Since 2013

Karina Waters bought Château de Gudanes in 2013, and has been bringing it back ever since. She is its Châtelaine.

A house like this was never meant to stand empty, so it is lived in while it is restored. Five bedrooms are open to guests, and the workshops bring people to stay, cook and take part in the house as it comes back.

La châtelaine The châtelaine

Owner and Châtelaine

Karina

Karina leads the restoration and the care of the house, room by room, as its owner and châtelaine. Every decision about the walls, the rooms and the guests who come to stay passes through her.
