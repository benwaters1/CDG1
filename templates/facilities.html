Skiing at Ax 3 Domaines

Twenty-five minutes from the gates. Eighty kilometres of piste between 1,400 and 2,400 metres, twenty-one lifts, and three linked domains — Bonascre, Saquet and Les Campels. Green through black, a snowpark, a boardercross, and two dedicated beginner areas.

A gondola runs from the centre of Ax-les-Thermes to the slopes in under ten minutes, so there is no mountain road to drive in the morning. The climb has finished a Tour de France stage five times.

We arrange the whole of it. Lift passes bought and in your hand before you leave. Transport there and back each day. Hire fitted at the resort — skis, boards, boots, poles, helmets — and lessons with the ESF or ESI schools if you would like them, for any age or level.

Tell us when you book and it is simply organised. You come down to breakfast and the day is already arranged.

Gudanes is a Class I Historic Monument, which means the walls stay as the centuries left them — bare plaster, weathered stone, protected by law and not ours to change. Everything within them is new: antique French beds dressed in vintage monogrammed linen, floors relaid by hand, bathrooms finished to a standard the rest of the château has not reached yet. The imperfection is deliberate, and staying here pays for the next room.
