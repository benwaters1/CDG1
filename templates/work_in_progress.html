These are photographs from the work itself, put up as it happens rather than chosen afterwards. Some of them are of a wall with nothing on it. That is the point: a Class I monument cannot be renovated in the ordinary sense, so the building comes back one approved intervention at a time, and most weeks what there is to show is a stripped reveal, a lifted floor, or a room with a bucket in it.

Nothing here is staged and nothing is a plan. It is what the château looked like on the day. The written record of finished work is here.
