Faults

Something a guest broke or took. Not the same as a fault — a dripping shower needs a plumber and is nobody's fault; this carries a question a fault never does. Everything is recorded whether or not anything is said about it, because three broken glasses across a season is wear and three in one stay is a conversation, and neither is visible unless both are written down.
