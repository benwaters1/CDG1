Before You Write

Can we visit?
The château is a private home under restoration and is not open to the public. It can only be seen as a staying guest, on a workshop, or at a private event.
Is it finished?
No, and it will not be for years. Restoring a Class I Historic Monument means every intervention is approved individually. Guests stay in a house that is still being made.
What about the walls?
Bare plaster and weathered stone are protected and cannot be changed. What is within them — beds, linen, floors, bathrooms, food — is new.
Children?
Welcome on nightly stays. Workshops are adult company by nature, though we will talk it through if you are asking.
Dogs?
Ours are in residence. Yours, sadly, cannot be — the restoration areas make it unsafe.
Accessibility
An eighteenth-century house with original staircases and no lift. Do write before booking and we will be honest about what is manageable.
Electricity
Limited by design. Rooms are lit by candle in the evening, and there are power outlets and bedside lamps.
How far is it?
Toulouse airport is about an hour and three-quarters. Les Cabannes station is three minutes from the gates.

Gudanes is a Class I Historic Monument, which means the walls stay as the centuries left them — bare plaster, weathered stone, protected by law and not ours to change. Everything within them is new: antique French beds dressed in vintage monogrammed linen, floors relaid by hand, bathrooms finished to a standard the rest of the château has not reached yet. The imperfection is deliberate, and staying here pays for the next room.
