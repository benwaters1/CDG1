The Journey

Getting Here

By car

An hour and three quarters from Toulouse-Blagnac. The last four kilometres climb and are unlit: if you are arriving after dark, telephone when you leave Les Cabannes and someone will meet you at the gates

By train

To Les Cabannes, three minutes from the gates by car

Further afield

Andorra, about an hour and a half; Carcassonne, about an hour and forty minutes; Barcelona, about three hours
