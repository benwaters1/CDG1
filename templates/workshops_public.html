Château Workshops

Three ways to spend time inside a working restoration — raw, unfinished, and utterly alive — mixed with the real luxury of French food, antiques and craft.

Just Beyond the Gate

The Ariège

The château sits deep in the Ariège — Cathar country, at the foot of the Pyrénées, in a corner of France that still feels wonderfully undiscovered.

Between Workshop activities, guests wander the antique fairs and vide-greniers of Mirepoix, one of France's best-preserved medieval bastide towns; browse the Sunday morning market at Les Cabannes for cheese, honey and whatever the season offers; and, on the longer stays, descend into the Grotte de Niaux, where paintings on the cave walls have survived some fourteen thousand years.

Afternoons might mean a guided hike into the high valleys, a paddle down the Ariège river, or simply a glass of local wine on the terrace as the light shifts on the mountains — this is rural France as the French themselves still live it: unhurried, deeply local, and entirely real.

Your Stay

The rooms, and the table

Each château bedroom welcomes you with fresh garden flowers and the calming scent of lavender. Beds are dressed in vintage monogrammed linens, ruffled pillows and floral sheets, with antique furnishings lending a refined, historic feel.

Rooms are arranged for two guests, or three by request, each offering up to 70 m² of comfort and privacy. Bathrooms are shared and beautifully appointed — the château is still under restoration, and ensuite bathrooms are not permitted under historic preservation rules.

Every day brings a new dining experience — leisurely breakfasts, garden picnics, alfresco lunches and elegant candlelit dinners, all prepared by our château chefs using regional, seasonal ingredients drawn from the Ariège's own markets and producers: mountain cheeses, foie gras, magret de canard, wild mushrooms in season, and desserts drawn from recipes in the château's own eighteenth-century cookbook.

A private selection of local wines, spirits and non-alcoholic drinks is offered with every meal.

Arrival & Departure

Getting here

On the first day we meet you at 11am in the centre of Toulouse, collect your luggage and travel to the château together.

At the end of your stay we leave the château at 11am, arriving in Toulouse around 1pm — we can drop you at the airport, the train station, or the centre of town. Private transport outside these times can be arranged at an additional cost.

Inclusions & Amenities

What the price carries

  • Daily dining prepared by the château chefs — from relaxed family-style meals to refined French dining, showing regional specialities and seasonal ingredients
  • Unlimited château beverages — wine, spirits, local beer and non-alcoholic options
  • Double-occupancy accommodation, triple by request, in an antique French bed, with a beautifully appointed shared bathroom — shower, WC and basin, and a freestanding bath in one of them
  • All activities as per the itinerary
  • Full use of château amenities — tennis, pickleball, pool and Wi-Fi
  • Laundry facilities
  • Transfers to and from Toulouse at the designated times

Travel insurance is required. A deposit reserves your place; the balance is due thirty days before the stay.
