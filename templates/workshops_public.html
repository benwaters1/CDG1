Les retraites

La Vie de Château

A few days of French château life, in rooms brought back from ruin.

See every date Compare with a stay

This is not a hotel stay, and it is not a course. It is the life of a French château as it was meant to be lived: unhurried mornings, long lunches, the market and the brocante, the kitchen, the apéritif, and dinner that lasts the evening.

You stay within the château itself, with a small company of guests and no one else. Each retreat has its theme: the kitchen, Christmas, the brocantes, the old crafts, or simply a French summer. Around it, the château does what it was built to do. Take part in everything, or nothing at all.

À table The table

The Table

In France, the table is the point of the day, and so it is here. The château’s chefs cook the classics of the French table. There is bread from the village boulangerie each morning, trout from the farm right beside the château, cheeses from the Ariège, produce from the morning’s market, pastries from our own pastry kitchen, and now and then a dish from the château’s eighteenth-century cookbook.

Some meals are long and easy: platters passed down a table in the garden, the barbecue on the terrace, a kitchen supper of what you cooked that afternoon. Most evenings are an occasion. Several courses are served by candlelight, perhaps a fillet of beef, a ballotine of guinea fowl or the trout from next door, with the silver laid and flowers down the table. There is local sparkling wine as you arrive, the wines of France at every meal, an apéritif at dusk, and nothing to pay at the end.
