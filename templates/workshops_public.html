Les ateliers The workshop retreats

Why a Retreat

The Best Way to Experience Gudanes

A night here gives you the house for an evening. A workshop retreat gives you the whole experience: the table every day, the house to yourselves, and the pace of château life for long enough to feel it.

It is a small group, and it is unhurried: a retreat, not a course. The sessions give the days their shape; the rest is the pool, the grounds, good food and wine, and conversation that runs on long after dinner.

Nobody arrives knowing how, and nobody needs to. The experience is made for curiosity, not expertise.

You are not only a guest here. You are part of how the house comes back: every retreat helps bring it back to life.
