Château de Gudanes

Château Workshops

Three ways to spend time inside a working restoration — raw, unfinished, and utterly alive — mixed with the real luxury of French food, antiques and craft.

Each Workshop welcomes a small group of guests into the château itself: to hunt for treasures at the brocantes, to cook alongside our chefs, or to help bring her eighteenth-century frescoes back to life.

Choose the length that suits you. Each stay includes full board, château beverages, shared accommodation and a curated itinerary — and every visit helps fund the restoration itself: a decade-long labour of love that has been peeling back the centuries, room by room, fresco by fresco, since the work began in 2013.

Included in Every Workshop

One price, and nothing added to it once you are here.

Full Board

Every meal prepared by the château chefs — long breakfasts, garden lunches, candlelit dinners. Unlimited château beverages throughout: wine, spirits, local beer and soft drinks.

Your Room

An antique French bed dressed in vintage monogrammed linen, and a beautifully appointed shared bathroom on the ground floor. Laundry whenever you need it.

Every Activity

The whole itinerary, and the house with it — tennis, pickleball, the pool, six acres of parkland, and Wi-Fi where you need it.

Getting Here

We meet you at 11am in central Toulouse and travel to the château together. At the end we leave at 11am and have you back in Toulouse by about one.

Group size
Small — the house takes no other guests while a workshop runs
Rooms
Arranged for two, a third bed on request. Solo travellers paired, or given a room where one is free
