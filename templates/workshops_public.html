Les ateliersThe workshops

Why a Workshop

The Best Way to Experience Gudanes

A night here gives you the house for an evening. A workshop gives you the whole experience: the table every day, the house to yourselves, and the pace of château life for long enough to feel it.

It is a small group, and it is unhurried. The sessions give the days their shape; the rest is the pool, the grounds, good food and wine, and conversation that runs on long after dinner.

Nobody arrives knowing how, and nobody needs to. The experience is made for curiosity, not expertise.

You are not only a guest here. You are part of how the house comes back: every workshop helps bring it back to life.

Les trois filsThe three threads

What You Do

Three Threads, Every Workshop

Every workshop weaves the same three together. The length decides which leads.

  • {{ illus('brocante') }}

    La Brocante

    The hunt

    The Ariège is a country of brocantes and antique fairs, and a good one is a morning well spent: the stalls, the bargaining, the piece you did not know you were looking for. The brocante workshop puts them at the centre; every workshop has a taste of them.

  • {{ illus('cuisine') }}

    La Cuisine

    The kitchen

    French cooking at the château, without hurry, and the table at the end of it. The cooking workshop puts the kitchen at the centre; every workshop eats well, with every meal and drinks included.

  • {{ illus('restoration') }}

    La Restauration

    The house

    A Class I Historic Monument coming back to life, seen up close: how the work is done, why the walls are left as they are, and what the house gives up as it returns. The restoration workshop puts it at the centre.

Une journéeA day

The Rhythm

A Day at the Château

Mornings begin slowly, with breakfast and the day ahead. The session takes the morning: the brocantes, the kitchen or the restoration, depending on the day. Lunch is together, and the afternoon is free time, yours to spend as you choose: a glass of local wine on the terraces, a game of tennis or padel, a swim in the pool, or nothing at all.

Before dinner there is an apéritif in the salon. Dinner is the hour nearly everybody keeps, and the conversation tends to run on long after it.

Along the way, and every one of them optional: morning yoga, kayaking or hiking in the valley as the season allows, a barbecue in the grounds, and a formal dinner in the Salon de Musique, by candlelight.

Chez vousAt home

Yours Alone

The House to Yourselves

While a workshop runs, the château is given over to it: no other guests, and no strangers at the next table. For a few days it is your house, and the people around you are the group you came with.
