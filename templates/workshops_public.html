The Ariège, Just Beyond the Gate

The château sits deep in the Ariège — Cathar country, at the foot of the Pyrénées, in a corner of France that still feels wonderfully undiscovered.

Between Workshop activities, guests wander the antique fairs and vide-greniers of Mirepoix, one of France's best-preserved medieval bastide towns; browse the Sunday morning market at Les Cabannes for cheese, honey and whatever the season offers; and, on the longer stays, descend into the Grotte de Niaux, where paintings on the cave walls have survived some fourteen thousand years.

Afternoons might mean a guided hike into the high valleys, a paddle down the Ariège river, or simply a glass of local wine on the terrace as the light shifts on the mountains — rural France as the French themselves still live it: unhurried, deeply local, and entirely real.

Your Stay

Each château bedroom welcomes you with fresh garden flowers and the calming scent of lavender. Beds are dressed in vintage monogrammed linens, ruffled pillows and floral sheets, with antique furnishings lending a refined, historic feel. Rooms are arranged for two guests, or three by request, each offering up to 70 m² of comfort and privacy.

Bathrooms are shared and beautifully appointed — the château is still under restoration, and ensuite bathrooms are not permitted under historic preservation rules.

Every day brings a new dining experience — leisurely breakfasts, garden picnics, alfresco lunches and elegant candlelit dinners, all prepared by our château chefs using regional, seasonal ingredients drawn from the Ariège's own markets and producers: mountain cheeses, foie gras, magret de canard, wild mushrooms in season, and desserts drawn from recipes in the château's own eighteenth-century cookbook.

A private selection of local wines, spirits and non-alcoholic drinks is offered with every meal.

Inclusions & Amenities

Dining, every day

Prepared by the château chefs — from relaxed family-style meals to refined French dining, showing regional specialities and seasonal ingredients.

Unlimited château beverages

Wine, spirits, local beer and non-alcoholic options.

Accommodation

Double occupancy, triple by request, in an antique French bed, with a beautifully appointed shared bathroom — a shower, toilet and basin, and a freestanding bath in one.

Every activity on the itinerary

Together with full use of the château amenities — tennis, pickleball, pool and Wi-Fi.

Transfers

To and from Toulouse at designated times, and laundry facilities throughout.

A hand in the restoration

The opportunity to take part in restoring the château's eighteenth-century frescoes.

Each Workshop excludes flights to and from Toulouse, personal spending money and travel insurance. Travel insurance is required for all guests.

Arrival & Departure

On the first day we meet you at eleven in the centre of Toulouse, collect your luggage and travel to the château together. At the end of your stay we leave the château at eleven, arriving in Toulouse around one — we can set you down at the airport, the station, or in the centre. Private transport outside these times can be arranged at additional cost.

Payment

A ten per cent deposit reserves your place, taken by card in Euros. The balance falls due sixty days before your stay, with a payment link sent beforehand — you are welcome to pay in instalments at your own discretion ahead of that date. Bookings made within sixty days of the stay are payable in full.
