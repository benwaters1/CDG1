Held, not earned Money ahead Financials

Revenue already sold for months that have not happened, counted in the month it is earned rather than the month the money arrives. A deposit taken in January for an August week is January’s cash and August’s trading. Money ahead asks the cash question about the same euros. Do not add the two together.

The other side of it: of what is sold here, whatever the guest has already paid is in the bank but not yet yours — owed back if the stay does not happen. That is Held, not earned, the figure the accountant asks for.
