No money on this page, deliberately. Hours past the standard week are majorées under French law, but pay rates here are free text and most people on the books have none, so any euro figure would be invented rather than calculated. The hours are real; a cost would not be. Your accountant works from the payroll pack, not from here.

What was on is shown beside the hours, not as an explanation of them. A busy week and a long week appearing together does not prove one caused the other — it is here so somebody who knows the house can recognise a pattern the app cannot.
