Booking.com prices

Download the list Stay occasions

Booking.com takes prices only from a channel manager, so the prices your occasions set are entered in its extranet by hand. Booking.com sells each room at two rates, Non-Refundable and the Standard Rate ({{ std_terms }}), both above the house's own price. On an occasion each goes up by the amount the occasion adds on the house site, which each line says. Each line here is one to enter: a room, its nights (the first and the last both included) and its two new prices, in euros. Once it is in, press Mark done. A line not yet done follows the occasion if you change it, and the room's Booking.com prices if you change those below; a line done stays as it was entered, and any change after it is a new line. Nothing here signs in to Booking.com.
