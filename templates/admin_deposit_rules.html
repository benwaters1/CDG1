One place to see every deposit rule across restaurant and workshop bookings. A rule can be scoped to a date range, a minimum party size, or both — the most specific matching rule wins. A booking that matches no rule falls back to that category's base deposit percentage below.

Base deposit percentages (the fallback)

Room stays: {% if room_percent %}{{ '%g'|format(room_percent) }}% up front, rest due {{ room_days }} days before arrival{% else %}charged in full online{% endif %}

A stay is often booked months ahead. At 0% the whole amount is taken at checkout, which is how it has always worked here. Set a percentage and the guest pays that now and the rest before they travel — they are reminded automatically, and the balance shows on their own page. Bookings already taken keep the terms they were made on.
