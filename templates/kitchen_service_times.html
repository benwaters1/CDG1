A night at a time, not one average over the month — a mean hides the two evenings that were actually bad, which are the only ones worth talking about. The walk from the pass to the table sits in its own column because it is a different problem from a slow kitchen, and it is the kitchen that gets blamed for it.

None of this judges anything. A slow night might be one table of twelve, and a long ticket might be a steak somebody sent back. The figures are here; the reading belongs to whoever was in the room.
