Guest register

The fiche individuelle de police, which an établissement d'hébergement must keep for guests who are not French nationals and produce if it is asked for. Only the fields the arrêté lists are here — no passport number, no scan of anything. Entries are deleted {{ keep_months }} months after the stay ends, automatically, by the same nightly job that keeps the rest of the promises in our privacy notice.
