Le tournage The shoot

How a Shoot Works

  1. I

    Tell us

    Your dates, the size of your crew, and what you need the rooms to do: power, a room darkened for filming, vehicle access.

  2. II

    Come and see

    A recce by appointment, before you book a day. Better to see the scaffolding now than on the morning of the shoot.

  3. III

    Agreed in advance

    The château is a Class I Historic Monument, so every shoot is agreed with us beforehand, and priced on request.

  4. IV

    The château is yours

    Only one event runs at the château at once, and the crew can stay on site or close by.
