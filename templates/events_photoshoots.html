What Is Here

Rooms Between Ruin and Restored

Ninety-four rooms across 43,000 square feet. Five are finished. The others range from stripped stone to salons with their gilding intact, which is a range of sets that cannot be built and cannot be dressed to look real.

The façade was designed by Ange-Jacques Gabriel, principal architect to Louis XV. The house has been photographed for Vogue, AD Italia and House & Garden, and classified a Class I Historic Monument since 14 June 1994.

The Constraints

What Class I Listing Means For a Crew

Nothing may be fixed to the historic fabric. No drilling, no adhesive on plaster or panelling, no rigging from the mouldings. Freestanding rig only.

We would rather set that out now than have it discovered on the morning of a shoot. Within it, the house is unusually accommodating: whole floors can be given over, and there is no other party to work around.
