The site's photographs

Change a picture Go-live checklist

Every photograph on the public pages — and the logo at the top of all of them — is loaded from a Squarespace account the château no longer publishes from. Nothing is wrong with that until the day the account lapses, and then the site loses all of them in the same minute. This page takes a copy of each one onto the château's own server and serves that copy instead. The pages are not edited to do it, so the next redesign cannot undo it.

This is not the page for changing a picture — that is Change a picture, where each place on the site is named and you upload what should be there. This page is about not losing the ones nobody has changed.
