The Record · Since 2013

The château, as it is

Photographs taken across the seasons, mostly by guests and by the family, over a decade of work.

There is no styling here and very little tidying. Rooms are photographed as they are on the day — some restored, some with the plaster still open and the scaffolding up. That is the point of the record.

Since 2013

Rooms That No Longer Look Like This

Photographs taken since 2013, of rooms in states that have gone and will not come back. A wall opened, a floor lifted, a ceiling before it was consolidated. Once a room is restored, that version of it exists only here.

It is the reason the record was kept at all. It is also, unexpectedly, the most useful thing on this site for anyone scouting a location — the same room can be seen stripped to stone and restored, which is a choice no other building can offer.

For anyone reading along

The archive is the long version of the journal: the house room by room, as it was found and as it comes back.

For location scouts

Tell us the condition you need and we will send what we have of it — bare stone, open plaster, part-restored, or fully restored. Usage is agreed in writing before a shoot, not after.
