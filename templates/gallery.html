Château de Gudanes

Inside the Château, As She Stands Today

These are recent photographs, not archive images — the kitchen, dining rooms, bedrooms and bathrooms exactly as guests find them now. A restoration is never finished, and neither is she: each room carries the marks of eighteenth-century craftsmanship alongside the quiet, ongoing work of bringing her back to life, fresco by fresco, floorboard by floorboard.

Everything you see below is lived-in and in use — antique beds guests sleep in tonight, a kitchen our chefs cook from this week, bathrooms fitted only where the historic fabric of the building allows it. Nothing staged, nothing borrowed. Just the château, honestly.
