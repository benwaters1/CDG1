Setting it up in Booking.com
  1. Extranet → your account → Contacts: give the Reservations topic this mailbox (and Invoices and Essential communications if you want those here too).
  2. Account → Notification settings: choose email for new reservations, changes, cancellations and new guest messages.
  3. Property → Messaging preferences → Security settings: add @chateaugudanes.com to the approved email addresses — Booking.com drops, without a word, any email to a guest from an address not on that list, so answering from here depends on it — and chateaugudanes.com to the approved links, or links to the site arrive as "[Link was removed]".
