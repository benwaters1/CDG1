"A while" means longer than {{ stale_days }} days and nothing more — plenty of things are issued permanently and are meant to stay out. It is here so a loan nobody remembers making surfaces while somebody still remembers making it.

There is no due-back date on either register, so nothing here can tell a permanent issue from a contractor's loan that should have come back in March. Adding one is the obvious next improvement, and until then the age column is standing in for it.
