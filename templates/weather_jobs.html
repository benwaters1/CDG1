Weather jobs

Maintenance Automation

The jobs that only count if they are done before the weather arrives. When the forecast crosses a line below, each job on that list becomes a task for the day it needs doing, on the calendar and on that person's Today list, saying what was forecast. Weather arriving before {{ morning }}:00 is prepared for the evening before, so a frost at six on Thursday is Wednesday evening's job. If the forecast changes and the weather is no longer coming, the jobs close on their own and say why; so does one whose day went by.
