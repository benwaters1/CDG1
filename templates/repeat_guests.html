Worth to the house is rooms, the table and ateliers together — somebody who stays one night and eats here four times is a regular by any reading that matters. It is what was charged, not what was collected; an unpaid balance still shows here and is chased on Money ahead.

Guests are matched by email address. Somebody who books under a work address one year and a personal one the next is two people to this page, and there is no way for the app to know otherwise — so a regular you recognise and this list does not is worth a moment rather than a correction.
