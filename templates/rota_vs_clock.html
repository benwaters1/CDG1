The rota is what somebody intended. The clock is what payroll actually pays from. They have never been compared, so when they disagree nobody finds out — and it costs something in both directions.

This says the records disagree, not that anybody did anything wrong

A shift with no clock entry is at least as likely to be a forgotten button as an absence — and if they did work it, those hours will never be paid. A clock entry with no shift may be somebody covering at short notice that nobody wrote down. Either way the useful fact is the same: these two records disagree, and one of them is wrong.
