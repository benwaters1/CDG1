Getting Here

Paris, Toulouse, and up the valley to the château.

The journey in one line

Paris → about 4 h 35 by fast train → Toulouse → an hour and three quarters by road, or about 1 h 49 by the regional train → Les Cabannes → three minutes → the château.

By train

The easy way for guests without a car. Take the fast train from Paris to Toulouse, then the regional train up the Ariège valley, towards Latour-de-Carol, as far as Les Cabannes, three minutes from the château. Tell us your train and someone will meet it: there is no taxi rank.

Tickets for both trains are on SNCF Connect. Coming from London, the Eurostar reaches Paris first.

By air and car

Fly to Toulouse-Blagnac. The château is an hour and three quarters away by road: the A61 and the A66, then the N20 up the valley through Foix and Tarascon, left at Les Cabannes, and the last few hundred metres up to the gates. Car hire is at the airport.

Make a trip of it

For a longer journey, one way to put it together, each stop with the time from the one before:

  1. Paris, for a few nights.
  2. The château: about 4 h 35 by fast train to Toulouse, then about 1 h 49 up the valley to Les Cabannes [2].
  3. Carcassonne, the walled city: about an hour and forty minutes by road.
  4. Toulouse, for the flight home: an hour and three quarters from the château by road.

Times by road are the ones on our route map; times by train are SNCF Connect's.

Good to know

  • The mountain road. The last stretch from Les Cabannes climbs and narrows; take it slowly. The drive is unlit: arriving after dark, telephone as you reach Les Cabannes and someone will meet you at the gates. Do not search the address: use the pin in your confirmation, as a search can send you to the village square instead of the gates.
  • In winter. From November to March, mountain roads in France ask for winter tyres or snow chains in the car; ask your hire company for them. The slopes are open from December to March.
  • Signal. The valley has patchy mobile signal. There is Wi-Fi in a common area of the château.
  • Supplies. Les Cabannes has its market on a Sunday, and Tarascon on Wednesday and Saturday.
  • Stairs. There is no lift: every bedroom is reached by stairs, and they are steep. Pack lightly.
