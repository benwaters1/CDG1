Who Comes

Is It for You?

If you love old houses, good food and the French way of taking time over things, yes. Nobody needs to know how; the experience is made for curiosity, not expertise.

Couples, friends travelling together and those who come alone are all equally at home. The group is small, the days are unhurried, and the house is yours.
