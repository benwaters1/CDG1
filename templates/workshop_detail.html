The Rhythm of Château Life

There is an itinerary. There is no obligation to it.

Only a small number of guests join each workshop, so the days stay unhurried. There is time to slow down, to settle in, and to let the château set the pace rather than a schedule.

Whilst there is an itinerary, there is no expectation to follow it. Join an experience, gather at the table with the others, or spend an afternoon beside the pool. The rhythm of your days is entirely your own.

Wander through centuries of history, walk beneath ancient trees, or lose yourself in the gardens and salons that have become the heart of the château.

Above all, the château is meant to feel lived in — a place to embrace each day exactly as you wish.

Guest Quarters

Les Chambres

Each bedroom is prepared with antique French beds, vintage monogrammed linens, heirloom furnishings and objects collected through the restoration. Beyond the windows, the mountains and the valley.

Guests are welcomed to their own antique French double bed; couples enjoy a private room with a king bed. Rooms are arranged for two, with a third available on request. Solo travellers are paired thoughtfully with a like-minded guest, or given a private room where one is free.

Bathing

Les Salles de Bains

Beautifully appointed with elegant fittings, floral wallpapers, soft towels and timeless details — a welcoming place to refresh and unwind.

As a protected eighteenth-century monument, the château has been restored under historic preservation guidelines. To honour its original architecture, ensuite bathrooms are not permitted; the bathrooms sit together on the ground floor, each privately allocated, with a dressing room adjoining.
