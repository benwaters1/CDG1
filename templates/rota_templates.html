Standard weeks

The rota

Not the same thing as copying last week. Last week had somebody off sick, a wedding on the Saturday and two people doubled up for it — copy that forward and by August the rota is a photocopy of one odd week in May. A standard week is the week you meant: high season, low season, the weeks the house is shut and the builders are in.
