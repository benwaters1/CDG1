The picture designs

Every way a photograph can now be set on a page, each with sample photographs. Nothing on the real pages has changed yet. Captions are shown here so the labels can be judged; on the site they stay hidden, as they have been since 15 October, unless you say otherwise.

A · Le cadre

The gilt frame

A single signature picture hung like a painting: gilt moulding, a dark line, a cream mount, a brass plaque. Three shapes: the arched top of the tall windows, the oval, and the rectangle.

{{ cadre([R ~ 'gingerbread-under-lights.jpg', 'The gingerbread château under the lights'], plaque='Le pain d’épices · 2025', shape='arch') }} {{ cadre([R ~ 'pastry-chef.jpg', 'The pastry chef in her whites'], plaque='La pâtissière', shape='oval') }} {{ cadre([R ~ 'chateau-at-night.jpg', 'The château at night'], plaque='Le château, la nuit', shape='rect') }}

B · Le passe-partout

The museum mount

Quieter than the frame. A wide cream mount, a hairline of gilt, and the caption set like a label in a museum. For food, objects and the finds.

{{ passe_partout([R ~ 'pocket-watches.jpg', 'Pocket watches on a stall', 'Pocket watches, found at the brocante']) }} {{ passe_partout([R ~ 'croquembouche.jpg', 'A croquembouche', 'A croquembouche, for the last night']) }} {{ passe_partout([R ~ 'apothecary-jars.jpg', 'Apothecary jars in a glass cabinet', 'Apothecary jars in a glass cabinet']) }}

C · L’album

The scrapbook

Two or three photographs in white borders, tilted and overlapping, held by paper corners, with a caption in the italic hand. For guests, evenings and friends. On a phone they stack, still a little tilted.

{{ album([[R ~ 'guests-toasting.jpg', 'Guests raising a glass', 'Raising a glass'], [R ~ 'guests-round-the-table.jpg', 'Guests round the table', 'Round the table'], [R ~ 'guests-with-finds.jpg', 'Guests with their finds', 'The day’s finds']]) }}

D · Le carrousel

The slider

One photograph at a time with the next one peeking in, a counter, and thin gilt arrows. It swipes on a phone, answers the arrow keys, and never moves on its own. For the rooms, a retreat’s best moments and the table.

The collage

G · La bande

The film strip

A strip of small photographs drifting slowly sideways. It stops under the pointer, and for anyone who has asked their device for less motion it does not move at all and scrolls by hand instead. It replaces the repeated band of four.

{{ bande(EVENINGS, 'The evenings') }}

Or a contact sheet, with the sprocket holes and frame numbers, for the photographs of the house as it was found.

{{ bande(FOUND, 'As it was found', mode='contact') }}

H · Le split

Words and a picture

{% call split([R ~ 'christmas-supper.jpg', 'Christmas supper in the kitchen'], tuck=[R ~ 'vin-chaud.jpg', 'Vin chaud on a silver tray']) %}

The default way to illustrate a paragraph: the words on one side, the photograph on the other, swapping sides each time it is used down a page.

On a wide screen the photograph steps out of the column towards the edge of the page, and a second, smaller one can be tucked over a corner. On a phone the photograph follows the words.

{% endcall %} {% call split([R ~ 'mountain-walk.jpg', 'Walking in the hills'], flip=True) %}

The next one swaps sides, so a page never reads as a column of photographs down one edge.

{% endcall %}

I · Plein cadre

The full-width band

One wide photograph across the whole page between sections, with a short line or a guest’s words over it. Where the browser can, the photograph moves a little slower than the page as you scroll.

{{ plein(['/static/img/rk/from-the-air-mountains.jpg', 'From the air, the mountains behind'], line='Each morning I woke up worried that it was only a dream.', by='A guest') }}

J · Le médaillon

The medallion

{{ medaillon([R ~ 'alfred-in-the-snow.jpg', 'Alfred in the snow']) }}

A small round or oval photograph in a gilt ring, set into the column of text so the words run round it. For Bruce and Alfred, the chef, a single dish or a detail.

It sits on either side, and on a phone it shrinks rather than taking a line of its own.

{{ medaillon([R ~ 'pastry-kitchen-with-bruce.jpg', 'In the pastry kitchen, with Bruce'], shape='oval', side='left') }}

The oval suits a portrait. Both keep the photograph’s own colours and put the gilt in the ring, not over the picture.

L · La carte postale

The postcard

The château’s old postcards as a design: a white border, a stamp in the corner made from the house’s own engraving, and a postmark across the edge. Sparingly: the history, the story and the restoration.
