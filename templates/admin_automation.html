Background jobs that run on their own — no need to keep the app open or set up an external cron service. Each one is safe to run more than once; running it manually below won't duplicate anything.

Auto-declines pending, unpaid room bookings the owner never acted on, and pre-generates the arrival-prep checklist for stays coming up in the next 2 days — runs every few minutes.

Sends the owner the same "what needs your attention" summary shown on the dashboard, roughly once every 24 hours.

Pulls in bookings from every connected Airbnb/Booking.com/VRBO calendar so nothing double-books.

Emails a confirmed workshop guest once, using the "Workshop: Balance due reminder" template, when their balance is due soon and still unpaid.

When a room, dinner reservation, or workshop registration is declined or cancelled, automatically emails any matching open waitlist entry instead of just flagging it for the owner to follow up manually.

Emails a confirmed workshop guest once, using the "Workshop: Feedback request" template, the day after their session ends — mirrors the room booking's checkout-time "How was your stay?" email.

{% if graph_enabled %}Scans the connected mailbox for messages unanswered too long, and for prices/dates mentioned in an email that don't match real pricing or availability. Clocking in/out is a deliberate action now, not a side effect of logging in/out — this catches the "forgot to clock out" case instead, closing a shift left open too long and notifying both the employee and the owner so it can be corrected.

Emails a full backup (database, uploaded documents and room photos) to the owner on a schedule, so a lost or corrupted Railway volume isn't the only copy. Falls back to the database alone if the full backup is too large to email. Same content as Admin → Backup, just sent automatically.
