The gap matters both ways. Over is unpaid overtime the house is accruing whether or not anybody has noticed — in France that is a liability, not a favour. Under is somebody on a thirty-five hour contract being given twenty, which is a pay cut nobody agreed to and the first thing they will raise. Hours are net of breaks, and a clock-out recorded before its clock-in cannot subtract from anybody’s total.
