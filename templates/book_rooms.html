Rooms at the Château

Reservations are made here, and confirmed by the château. Manage an existing booking

Seven bedrooms have been opened to guests, each one as the restoration reached it.

The walls around you are as the centuries left them. Under Class I protection they may not be touched, and much of the château remains bare plaster and weathered stone. Everything within those walls has been made anew — the beds and the linen, the new oak floors underfoot, and bathrooms finished to a standard the building deserves.

Our chef cooks from the Sunday market in the village square, the producers of the Ariège, and in summer the château's own gardens. Breakfast arrives with viennoiseries carried up from the boulangerie three minutes below, and dinner is served in a dining room that took years to recover.

Rooms are reserved here and confirmed by the château. Very few will ever stay within a restoration of this standing.

The Bathrooms

A château under restoration might suggest a certain hardship. Here it is quite the opposite — the bathrooms are the most modern rooms in the building, because they had to be.

Ensuites are not permitted. To cut them into the bedrooms would destroy the historic plaster, stonework and papers that have survived there. Instead they are gathered together in a central space on the ground floor, within a room already altered in the twentieth century — the one place where intervention costs the château nothing.

Each has a shower, washbasin and WC, and one a freestanding bath. There are two further powder rooms, on the ground and first floors, and a dressing room recovered as part of the same work — vanities, soft lighting, and somewhere to prepare for the evening that is not the end of a bed.

Chambre Marie-Louise Lachapelle has a half ensuite of its own, with washbasin and bath.

The Salons & Grounds

Salon de Rose

The drawing room. A library, comfortable seating, cards and jigsaws, and a writing desk with postcards, stamps and stationery.

Salon de Thé

Open from morning until evening. French and local teas, herbs from the château gardens, coffee, and hot water refreshed through the day.

La Piscine

Hidden within the gardens, with views across the valley to the mountains, surrounded by cherry and plum trees and raspberry bushes. Towels provided.

The Tennis Court

Secluded within the parkland. Rackets and balls for tennis and pickleball are kept at reception.

The Rhythm Of The House

  • ArrivalFrom two o'clock. Early arrival by request and subject to availability. Tell us when to expect you and we shall be waiting.
  • DepartureBy ten o'clock, with late departure by arrangement. The house keeps quiet hours between ten in the evening and eight in the morning.
  • BreakfastServed between eight and ten. Dietary requirements are designed into the menu rather than worked around on the night — please send them before you arrive.
  • ChildrenWarmly welcome. Cots and high chairs are provided with our compliments.
  • Finding usTwo hours from Toulouse. Les Cabannes lies three minutes' walk below — boulangerie, grocer, pharmacy, post office, and a market in the square on Sunday mornings.

What we would have you know

We would rather you arrived knowing precisely what awaits you. Gudanes is not a hotel, and she has never pretended to be.

What you will find

  • Bedrooms and bathrooms finished to a genuinely high standard
  • Meals drawn from the valley, the market and the gardens
  • Mountains, silence, and remarkably few other people
  • The freedom to walk into rooms still being worked upon

What you will not

  • A finished building — much of her remains bare
  • Ensuite bathrooms in most rooms; the law that protects her forbids them
  • Hotel uniformity, room service, or a front desk
  • Scaffolding hidden from view when work is underway

A house of this consequence deserves the longer course

Restoration is slower and more exacting than renovation, and Gudanes will be held to that standard for as long as the work takes. Guests are welcome throughout it — and are the reason the rooms are opened at all.
