Stay the Night

A rare invitation: stay inside a world-renowned restoration project, within a building still evolving, unmodernised, and utterly unlike anywhere else. This is the château as she truly is — raw, ongoing, alive with history still being uncovered.

Bathrooms

Three bathrooms on the ground floor, each with its own character.

The King Room, Suite with Mountain View and Family Suite each have a private bathroom nearby in the area. The Double and Twin/Double rooms share a beautifully appointed modern bathroom set between the two.

Ensuite bathrooms are not permitted under historic preservation rules — which is why they sit together, and why they are the most modern rooms in the building.

Château Estate

Yours simply by staying — nothing below is charged as an extra.

Outdoors

A pool set among the cherry trees below the formal gardens, and a tennis court secluded at the edge of the grounds. Table tennis and a small gym in the house.

Gardens & Parkland

Historic formal gardens nearest the house, then parkland running up toward the mountain. Walk it at any hour. A BBQ area for long evenings outside.

Inside the House

Continental breakfast each morning, tea and coffee through the day, and lounge areas to settle into. Laundry facilities whenever you need them.

Interiors & Details

Château de Gudanes is under restoration, having been a ruin until recent years — work is ongoing throughout the estate. Most bathrooms sit together in a central area, though each is privately allocated to its room, aside from the shared rooms, which share a bathroom between the two.

The château is home to one resident French Bulldog — naturally — and a few cats.

Estate grounds of approximately six acres, in the Ariège, at the foot of the Pyrénées.
