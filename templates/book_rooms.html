Château de Gudanes

Stay the Night

Seven bedrooms inside a world-renowned restoration — a Class I Historic Monument still being brought back, and open to guests while the work goes on.

The walls are bare plaster and weathered stone. They are protected under French law and cannot be changed, so they stay as two hundred and eighty years left them. Everything within them is new and was chosen carefully — the beds, the linen, the floors underfoot, the bathrooms.

Both things are true at once. It is a stay with real limitations: no lift, original staircases, shared bathrooms on the ground floor, and electricity kept deliberately low so the evenings are lit by candle. If that sounds like a compromise, it is the wrong house. If it sounds like the point, it is the right one.

Every night booked pays for the next room. That is not a donation — it is how the work continues.

Bathrooms

Three bathrooms on the ground floor, each with its own character.

The King Room, Suite with Mountain View and Family Suite each have a private bathroom nearby in the area. The Double and Twin/Double rooms share a beautifully appointed modern bathroom set between the two.

Ensuite bathrooms are not permitted under historic preservation rules — which is why they sit together, and why they are the most modern rooms in the building.

Interiors & Details

Château de Gudanes is under restoration, having been a ruin until recent years — work is ongoing throughout the estate. Most bathrooms sit together in a central area, though each is privately allocated to its room, aside from the shared rooms, which share a bathroom between the two.

The château is home to one resident French Bulldog — naturally — and a few cats.

Estate grounds of approximately six acres, in the Ariège, at the foot of the Pyrénées.
