Enquiries

Export this view

People who have asked about dates. Most come from the Contact page's "tell us roughly when" form; a few are guests who searched the booking page and found every room taken. Each is kept across the dates or the month they asked about, so a night that frees up there reminds you to write back. Contact enquiries wait as Enquiry rather than Open, so the letter that offers a freed room never goes to somebody who only mentioned a month. Answering one, or marking it Closed, ticks its task; Spam takes the task away and sets aside anything more from that address.
