Where costs land

Cost codes are set on the thing itself, so they follow it automatically: stock items carry the code for what they are, recurring costs carry their own, and an invoice can override both. {{ expense_accounts|length }} expense accounts are available to pick from.

Kinds of document

Not every bill is handled the same way, so each one is tagged when it arrives:

A bill we still owe Filed in Pennylane as an unpaid supplier invoice, ready to be paid.
Already paid Usually already in Pennylane from the bank feed. Should be matched and completed rather than sent again, or the cost is counted twice.
Staff expense to reimburse The cost belongs to the château but the money is owed to an employee, not a supplier.
Receipt / small purchase Photographed on the spot; often no formal invoice exists.
