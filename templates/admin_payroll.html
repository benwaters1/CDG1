Absence and leave are counted in calendar days overlapping this window. Gross comes from the wage on file where there is one — hours × rate for an hourly wage, the salary pro-rated across the window for a monthly one. Where there is no wage record it falls back to reading the free-text pay note, which is marked as such, because a number parsed out of a note somebody typed is not a payroll figure. Anyone with neither shows no gross; their hours are still correct.
