The French Table

La Table Française

French cooking is about time as much as taste: a meal that is not hurried, and a table that is not cleared too soon. Dinner at Gudanes is cooked that way, seasonal and generous, in a dining room brought back from ruin, and it begins, as it should, with an apéritif.

On a workshop every meal is included, with alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, mostly local: the table is at the heart of the full experience.
