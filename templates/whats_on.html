The Valley Through the Year

Spring
Wildflowers through the parkland, the gardens waking, and the markets filling again after winter. Snow still on the high peaks.
Summer
The pool opens. Long evenings, dinner outside, and the vide-greniers and antique fairs at their best — Mirepoix on a Monday, Foix at the end of July.
Autumn
Virginia creeper turns the gates scarlet. Mushrooms in the woods, the harvest through the valley, and the light that photographers come for.
Winter
Ax 3 Domaines is twenty-five minutes away — eighty kilometres of piste between 1,400 and 2,400 metres. The thermal baths at Ax run 33–38°C all year.

Worth the Drive

Mirepoix

One of the best-preserved medieval bastide towns in France, and the most vibrant market in the Ariège. Monday mornings under the timbered arcades. About an hour.

Grotte de Niaux

Paleolithic paintings on the cave walls, fourteen thousand years old and still vivid. Numbers are limited and must be booked ahead. Twenty minutes.

Château de Foix

A medieval fortress above the town, with a museum tracing the Counts of Foix, crossbow and forging demonstrations, and the valley laid out below. Forty minutes.

Gudanes is a Class I Historic Monument, which means the walls stay as the centuries left them — bare plaster, weathered stone, protected by law and not ours to change. Everything within them is new: antique French beds dressed in vintage monogrammed linen, floors relaid by hand, bathrooms finished to a standard the rest of the house has not reached yet. The imperfection is deliberate, and staying here pays for the next room.
