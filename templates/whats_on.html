Le pays The country

Ariège

L’Ariège

The Ariège is one of the quietest corners of France: the Pyrénées, Cathar castles on their peaks, caves painted in prehistory, spa towns and market squares, and brocantes through the summer.

The château is a base for all of it, and a place to come back to at the end of the day. Most of the workshops, the full experience, take you out to the fairs.

Places

Towns and Places Nearby

Tarascon-sur-Ariège · about fifteen minutes

The nearest town, with its markets and its cafés

Ax-les-Thermes · about twenty minutes

A spa town in the mountains, with skiing at Ax 3 Domaines in winter, about half an hour away

Niaux · about twenty minutes

Painted caves, and one of the few places in Europe where you can still see the original prehistoric paintings

The Plateau de Beille · up the château’s road, about twenty-five minutes

The first Nordic ski area in the Pyrénées, at 1,800 metres: cross-country skiing on some forty kilometres of trails, snowshoeing, sledging, and sled-dog rides with the mushers of Angaka. High pastures and forest walks in summer

Foix · about half an hour

The castle of the Counts of Foix, above the old town

{#- 6 Oct (owner: "include that Andorra is close"). About an hour: Château-Verdun to Pas de la Casa is c. 45 km (listings in the commune say 40-45 km, 45 min; Ariège Pyrénées Tourisme gives Ax-les-Thermes to Pas de la Casa as 31 km, 40 min). -#}

Andorra · about an hour

Up the valley past Ax to the border at Pas de la Casa: shops open almost every day of the year, and the Grandvalira ski area in winter

Mirepoix · just under an hour

A medieval square of timbered houses and covered arcades, one of the best brocante towns in the region, and a grand market every Monday morning

Montségur · about three quarters of an hour

The Cathar castle on its peak, and the last stand of the Cathars

À faire Things to do

Things to Do Nearby

Les Bains du Couloubret · Ax-les-Thermes, about twenty minutes

Roman-style thermal baths fed by the hottest springs in the Pyrénées: indoor and outdoor pools, a caldarium and a frigidarium, hammam, sauna and massages. Open all year, apart from two short closures

On the river · about ten minutes

Canoe, kayak or paddle-board on the Ariège with Waterploof, from the lake at Sinsat: with a guide or on your own, six or twelve kilometres downriver, and a minibus back. Every day from May to October

Canyoning · Tarascon and Ax-les-Thermes

Guided canyons with Wesh Canyon at Tarascon, or Adret Canyon’s hot-water canyon at Ax

Fly fishing · Larcat, a few minutes away

A day on the water with a guide from Hook A Lip

Caves and prehistory · about twenty minutes

Beyond Niaux, the caves of Bédeilhac and Lombrives, and the Parc de la Préhistoire at Tarascon

Le ski Skiing

Skiing Nearby

Ax 3 Domaines, above Ax-les-Thermes, is about half an hour from the château; the Plateau de Beille, at the top of the château’s road, is the first Nordic area in the Pyrénées, for cross-country skiing, snowshoes and sled dogs, about twenty-five minutes; there is snowshoeing too at the Chioula, above Ax; and Andorra is about an hour.

Les eaux chaudes The hot springs

The Hot Springs

Ax-les-Thermes has some sixty hot springs, the hottest in the Pyrenees, rising from the ground at over 70°C.

Les Bains du Couloubret · Ax-les-Thermes, about 20 minutes

Roman-style thermal baths filled with natural spring water at 33 to 38°C. Inside, a glass-roofed pool ringed with columns, with jets and bubble seats, a hot bath, a cold plunge and a thermal steam room. Outside, two pools with views of the mountains. Upstairs, for over-sixteens, saunas and a hammam. Open all year; children from five. Bring your own towel, and leave silver jewellery at home, as the water darkens it.

{{ site('https://acqs.com/', 'acqs.com') }}

The Bassin des Ladres · Ax-les-Thermes

A medieval stone basin in the middle of Ax where everyone stops to soak their feet in the hot spring water. Free, every day, all year. Test the water first; it is hot.

A thermal spa half-day · Ax-les-Thermes

For a taste of a real thermal cure, the Thermes d’Ax offer a three-hour “Découverte Aqua” session for adults: three treatments, then the thermal pool, jacuzzis, hammam and sauna. There is also an after-sport version for skiers and cyclists. Booking needed; closed on Sundays.

{{ site('https://acqs.com/', 'acqs.com') }}

Thermes d’Ussat-les-Bains · about 20 minutes

A small spa in a park near Tarascon, newly renovated, with single treatments and massages. Telephone before you go, as opening dates vary.

{{ site('https://www.thermes-ussat.fr/', 'thermes-ussat.fr') }}

Caldea · Andorra, about an hour and a half

See Andorra, below.

Dehors Outdoors and adventure

Outdoors and Adventure

Rock climbing · Lordat, 10–15 minutes · Calamès, 25 minutes

The valley is a climbers’ playground. The crag below Château de Lordat has about a hundred routes on pale, marble-like limestone. Calamès, near Tarascon, has more than two hundred routes, faces the sun and can be climbed all year. Beginners go out with a local guide.

Guides: Vertikarst ({{ site('https://www.vertikarst.fr/', 'vertikarst.fr') }}) · Caverne & Canyon ({{ site('https://cavernecanyon.com/', 'cavernecanyon.com') }}) · Montcalm Aventure ({{ site('http://www.montcalmaventure.com/', 'montcalmaventure.com') }})

Via ferrata · Vicdessos, about 40 minutes

Ladders, cables and rope bridges on the cliffs above the Vicdessos valley. It is the only via ferrata in the Ariège, with an easy route from age seven and harder ones from fourteen. Kit is hired on site.

Montcalm Aventure · {{ site('http://www.montcalmaventure.com/', 'montcalmaventure.com') }}

Canyoning · Auzat and Orlu, 30–40 minutes

Guided descents of the mountain gorges, with abseils, jumps and natural water slides. All the kit is provided, and there are trips for first-timers. Summer.

{{ site('https://www.vertikarst.fr/', 'vertikarst.fr') }} · {{ site('https://cavernecanyon.com/', 'cavernecanyon.com') }} · {{ site('https://acrobranche-ariege.com/', 'acrobranche-ariege.com') }}

Rafting, canoe and paddleboard · Bouan, 10–15 minutes

Down the Ariège by raft, canoe or kayak, guided or on your own, with a minibus back; paddleboards on the lake below the Sinsat cliffs. May to October.

Waterploof · {{ site('http://waterploof.com/', 'waterploof.com') }}

Paragliding · above Foix or Vicdessos, about an hour

Tandem first flights with an instructor from the mountain take-offs at Prat d’Albis and Port de Lers. Spring to autumn, weather permitting.

{{ site('https://www.kymaya.com/', 'kymaya.com') }} · {{ site('https://parapente-pyrenees-parapente-ariege.fr/', 'parapente-pyrenees-parapente-ariege.fr') }}

Mountain biking and the Beille climb · Ax and Beille, 20–35 minutes

In summer the Ax 3 Domaines bike park runs six downhill trails from the Saquet plateau to the village, with the gondola to take you up. Plateau de Beille has gentle mountain-bike loops. For road cyclists, the Tour de France climb to Beille starts at the foot of the château’s road. Bikes and e-bikes can be hired in Ax.

{{ site('https://www.ax.ski/', 'ax.ski') }} · {{ site('https://www.beille.fr/', 'beille.fr') }}

Horse riding · Orgeix, about 25 minutes

Rides and treks for every level on the local Mérens horses, and pony rides for small children, at a family farm in the Orlu valley. Booking needed.

{{ site('https://harasdescimes.com/', 'harasdescimes.com') }}

Treetop park and the wolves · Orlu, about 30 minutes

Akrobranch d’Orlu has fifteen treetop courses in a beech wood above a waterfall, from toddler trails to zip lines. Next to it, the Maison des Loups keeps European, Canadian and Arctic wolf packs in large woodland enclosures.

{{ site('https://acrobranche-ariege.com/', 'acrobranche-ariege.com') }} · {{ site('https://maisondesloups.com/', 'maisondesloups.com') }}

Summer at Ax 3 Domaines · about 20 minutes to the gondola

The gondola climbs from the middle of Ax to the Bonascre plateau, and a chairlift carries on to the Saquet viewpoint at about 2,000 metres. Up top: a mountain-kart run, an outdoor pool, mini-golf, donkey walks and a restaurant with the whole valley below. July and August.

{{ site('https://www.ax.ski/', 'ax.ski') }}

Walks to the high lakes · from 10 minutes

The mountains around the château are full of lakes. The Étangs de Fontargente, up the Aston valley, are a half-day loop to lakes at 2,145 metres. From Mérens, the walks to the Étang de Comte and the Étang des Bésines are easy family outings. The GR10 crosses the Plateau de Beille. Roughly June to October, depending on the snow.

Fishing

Rivers, mountain streams and high lakes. A permit is needed; buy one at {{ site('https://www.cartedepeche.fr/', 'cartedepeche.fr') }} or at the tourist office in Ax or Tarascon.

{{ site('https://www.peche-ariege.com/', 'peche-ariege.com') }}

L’hiver Winter

Winter

Ax 3 Domaines · about 20 minutes to the gondola

The valley’s main ski resort: 80 kilometres of runs between 1,400 and 2,400 metres. The gondola leaves from the centre of Ax-les-Thermes, so you can leave the car in town. Ski schools, a snowpark, sledging and snow-tubing. Early December to late March.

{{ site('https://www.ax.ski/', 'ax.ski') }}

Plateau de Beille · up the château’s road, about 35 minutes

Our own snow: a high plateau at 1,800 metres with forty kilometres of cross-country trails, snowshoe routes and sledging. The Angaka village there runs dog-sled and sleigh rides and night snowshoe walks. Mid-December to late March.

{{ site('https://www.beille.fr/', 'beille.fr') }} · {{ site('https://www.angaka.com/', 'angaka.com') }}

Smaller resorts

Ascou-Pailhères (about 45 minutes), a friendly forest resort above Ax · Le Chioula (about 40 minutes), quiet Nordic trails and dog sledding · Goulier (about 45 minutes), one of the smallest resorts in the Pyrenees, with no queues.

{{ site('https://www.ascou.ski/fr/', 'ascou.ski') }} · {{ site('https://www.chioula.fr/hiver', 'chioula.fr') }} · {{ site('https://www.goulier-neige.fr/', 'goulier-neige.fr') }}

After skiing

Wood-fired Nordic hot tubs and a sauna at the Bains de Bonascre, on the slopes at Ax (ski season, booking needed), or the thermal baths in Ax itself (see the hot springs).

Sous la terre The caves

The Caves

This valley has one of the richest groups of Ice Age sites in Europe, all within half an hour of the château. Book ahead in summer.

Grotte de Niaux · about 20 minutes

One of the very few caves in the world where you can still see the original prehistoric paintings, not copies. A guide leads you by hand-held lamp through vast dark galleries to the Salon Noir, where more than seventy bison, horses and ibex were painted some 17,000 years ago. The visit takes about an hour and three quarters, at 12°C; wear warm clothes and walking shoes. Numbers are strictly limited, so booking online is essential. Not suitable for under-sixes; no bags or photographs.

{{ site('https://www.sites-touristiques-ariege.fr/grotte-de-niaux/', 'sites-touristiques-ariege.fr') }}

Parc de la Préhistoire · Tarascon, about 20 minutes

The perfect partner to Niaux, and a favourite with children. Throw a spear, knap flint, make fire, and walk through a full-size copy of Niaux’s Salon Noir. Allow half a day. Spring to early November.

{{ site('https://www.sites-touristiques-ariege.fr/parc-de-la-prehistoire/', 'sites-touristiques-ariege.fr') }}

Grotte de Lombrives · Ussat, about 15 minutes

One of the largest caves in Europe, full of legend: prehistoric shelters, Cathar stories and the mythical tomb of Pyrène. The guided walk crosses vast chambers such as the Cathedral, and a little train takes you up the steep path to the entrance. Open all year, with night walks and candlelit concerts.

{{ site('https://www.grottedelombrives.com/', 'grottedelombrives.com') }}

Grotte de Bédeilhac · about 25 minutes

“The giant”: a huge entrance porch and vast chambers, with rare prehistoric art modelled in clay. During the Second World War aircraft were stored inside it. Easier than Niaux for families with younger children.

{{ site('https://www.sites-touristiques-ariege.fr/grotte-de-bedeilhac/', 'sites-touristiques-ariege.fr') }}

Grotte de la Vache · Alliat, about 20 minutes

A small cave across the valley from Niaux where the Magdalenians spent their winters; perhaps the home of Niaux’s painters. Sold as a combined ticket with Niaux.

{{ site('https://www.sites-touristiques-ariege.fr/grotte-de-la-vache/', 'sites-touristiques-ariege.fr') }}

Underground river of Labouiche · near Foix, about 45 minutes

A boat trip along an underground river sixty metres below the ground, past formations called the Witch and the Unicorn. Suits all ages. April to November and the Christmas holidays.

{{ site('https://www.labouiche.com/', 'labouiche.com') }}

Mas d’Azil · about an hour and ten minutes

The river and the road run straight through the hill under a seventy-metre arch, so you drive through the cave. It gave its name to a period of prehistory, the Azilian.

{{ site('https://www.sites-touristiques-ariege.fr/grotte-du-mas-dazil-2/', 'sites-touristiques-ariege.fr') }}

L’Andorre Andorra

Andorra

Getting there · about an hour

Up the valley through Ax and Mérens, then over the pass to Pas de la Casa on the border. From there the road climbs over the Port d’Envalira, the highest road in the Pyrenees, or through the Envalira tunnel down to Andorra la Vella, about an hour and a half from the château. Bring a passport or ID card: Andorra is outside the EU. From November to May carry snow chains unless the car has winter tyres.

Shopping

Low taxes have made Andorra a shopping trip for generations: Pas de la Casa right at the border, and the long shopping street through Andorra la Vella and Escaldes. Shops are open most days until 8pm.

Caldea · Escaldes-Engordany

The largest thermal spa in southern Europe, in a glass cathedral with an eighty-metre spire. Indoor and outdoor lagoons are fed by the local hot springs, including one with a glass floor over the river. Open every day; book ahead, and only on {{ site('https://www.caldea.com/en/', 'caldea.com') }}.

Grandvalira

The largest ski area in the Pyrenees, with 215 kilometres of runs. The Pas de la Casa side is about an hour from the château. Early December to early April.

{{ site('https://www.grandvalira.com/en', 'grandvalira.com') }}

In summer

Walk the Madriu-Perafita-Claror valley, a UNESCO World Heritage landscape with no roads, or visit the small Romanesque churches of Sant Joan de Caselles and Santa Coloma and the old stone parliament house, Casa de la Vall.

{{ site('https://visitandorra.com/en/', 'visitandorra.com') }}

Patrimoine Close by

Close By

Château de Lordat · 10–15 minutes

An eleventh-century fortress and Cathar refuge on a rock above our valley, with sweeping views, falconry shows and medieval days in season.

Talcaneô · Luzenac, about 10 minutes

A museum about the talc quarry on the mountain above, the largest in the world, with coach trips up to the quarry on weekdays from May to November.
