Dans la presse In the press

Since the first photographs of a roofless château in the Pyrénées appeared online in 2013, Gudanes has been followed by readers on every continent. Vogue has walked its corridors twice. Harper’s Bazaar has printed it across its pages. Architectural Digest asked why a château should need its own Instagram account, and then answered the question itself.

The story they return to is the one we live each day: an eighteenth-century château, designed by Ange-Jacques Gabriel, architect of the Petit Trianon, being given back its life one room at a time.

La médaille d’honneur The Medal of Honour

Recognition

The Medal of Honour

In 2016 the Fondation Prince Louis de Polignac awarded Château de Gudanes its Medal of Honour, in recognition of the restoration. It was presented by Prince Albert II of Monaco at the Institut de France in Paris.

Le livre The book

The Book

Château de Gudanes: A True Love Story Never Ends (2018) tells the story of the château, its past, present and future. Every copy sold goes towards the restoration.
