The Story

The Story the World Has Followed

Since 2013 the restoration of Gudanes has been followed the world over: in print, on screen, and by people who have never been and would love to come. It is a story about a house being given back its life, and it is still being written.

The Medal of Honour

In 2016 the Fondation Prince Louis de Polignac awarded Château de Gudanes its Medal of Honour, in recognition of the restoration, and it was presented by Prince Albert II of Monaco.
