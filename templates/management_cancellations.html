Why they cancel

Bookings

A guest cancelling because the price went up and a guest cancelling because their mother is ill were the same row. Asked, never required — a guest cancelling in a hurry does not owe the house an explanation, and a mandatory field collects a lie or an empty string. Most reasons arrive on the telephone, so they can be added afterwards.
