Should you take it at a discount?

Not selling a room saves €{{ '%.2f'|format(cost['avoidable_per_night']) }} a night, not €{{ '%.2f'|format(cost['per_night']) }}. Everything else is spent whether the room goes or not.

So a night sold above €{{ '%.2f'|format(cost['avoidable_per_night']) }} leaves the house better off than an empty one, and that is the honest floor — not a recommendation to sell there. Filling the house at that price means the standing costs are never covered, and the figure above is what they come to.
