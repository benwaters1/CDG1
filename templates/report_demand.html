These are not the occupancy figures on the reports page, and they are not meant to agree with them. That one divides by every active room times every night, which answers “how full was the house”. This divides by the nights a guest could actually have had — so a room off sale for plaster work, or a week held for an atelier, is not counted as a night the house failed to fill. Otherwise the number gets worse the more restoration happens.
