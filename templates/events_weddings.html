Le château entier The whole château

Weddings

The Whole Château, Not a Room In It

For the days of your wedding, the château and its grounds are yours alone.

Fifteen of you sleep under its roof; the rest of your guests are a few minutes away.

It is a restoration, not a renovation. The walls stay as the centuries left them, by law, and they are the setting.

Each wedding is priced on request: write to us with your dates and the size of your party.

Le grand jour The day itself

The Day

A Wedding in a Château

Say your vows in a château the world has watched come back to life, with the Pyrénées all around and no other celebration under its roof.

The setting asks for little decoration: old stone, candlelight, and the valley at every window. It is a day your guests will remember long after it ends.
