What You Take

The Whole House, Not a Room In It

One wedding runs at a time. For the days you have it, there are no other guests, no handover halfway through, and nobody else's party in the next room.

Ceremonies happen in the grounds or in the historic interiors. Dinner is in the main dining salon. Above forty, dinner moves outdoors — the terraces and the parkland take larger numbers comfortably.

Be Warned

It Is Not Finished, And That Is The Point

This is a restoration, not a renovation, running since 2013. Under Class I listing the walls stay as the centuries left them — bare plaster, weathered stone, protected by law.

What you and your guests touch is new: the beds, the linen, the floors, the bathrooms. What photographs is old. Couples who want a finished house should look elsewhere, and we would rather say so now than on the day.
