La table The table

Every Meal, Cooked Here

At the Château’s Tables

A welcome reception, long lunches, a barbecue on the terrace, dinners by candlelight and your wedding dinner: every meal is cooked and served by us at the château, where your whole party is welcome by day and evening. Only breakfast is taken by guests staying nearby at their own accommodation.

You create the menu with our gourmet chefs before you arrive, from seasonal and local produce, down to the wedding cake and the desserts. Local Ariégeois wine and crémant are served from the welcome reception to your wedding dinner, and the bar, with its spirits, is open to you and your guests throughout your stay, with choices without alcohol at every meal.

A Sample Celebration

Three nights, shaped entirely around your wishes. This is a sample only.

    The Day of Arrival

    • Welcome to the château with a sparkling toast; your other guests settle into their rooms nearby, with refreshments and flowers waiting

    La Fête de Mariage

    • A private breakfast for the two of you, and breakfast for the guests staying at the château in the Salon de Porcelaine

    • Dancing until midnight, indoors or under the stars
  3. III

    The Day After

    • A slow breakfast, and a long lunch on the terrace or a picnic in the gardens
    • A historic tour of the château’s salons
    • A farewell dinner under the chandeliers
  4. IV

    Au Revoir

    • A light breakfast before your departure
