What the house has already promised, against what it has already been promised. Every line is something somebody has agreed to: a booking taken, a cost on a contract, a shift on the rota. This is not a bank balance and not a forecast of trading. Walk-in restaurant and till takings are most of a quiet month's income and cannot be known in advance, so they are not here — a month showing a shortfall below may well trade out of it.

Rooms are the balance still outstanding on confirmed bookings, dated by when it falls due — the balance date where a deposit was taken, otherwise arrival. Ateliers are balances not yet paid, dated by when they fall due. Events are the quoted price of a confirmed enquiry, which is a quote rather than an invoice. Anything a guest has already paid is in the bank, not coming in, so it is not counted twice. Enquiries that are not confirmed are not here at all — a maybe is not money.
