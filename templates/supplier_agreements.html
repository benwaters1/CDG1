Recurring costs

Sorted by the last day notice can be given, not by the renewal date. An agreement that rolls over on the first of January with three months’ notice has to be cancelled by the first of October — by the time the renewal is close enough to notice, the decision was taken three months ago by nobody. That is the whole reason a diary does not catch this.
