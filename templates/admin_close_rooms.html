Nights another channel has blocked

Booking.com's calendar is read every hour, and a stay cancelled there reopens here at the next read — the waiting list is told, as it is for a cancellation on this site. Its calendar carries dates and nothing else, so only you can know a stay there can be cancelled for free. You can keep this site open for one: the channel is not told, and still sees the room as booked by its guest.
