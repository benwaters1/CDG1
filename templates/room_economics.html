What is in the room figure. The room's own portion only: extras bought alongside — hampers, transfers, activities — are in their own column and are not the bedroom earning money. Discounts are already off, because they were genuinely given away. City tax never appears anywhere here; it is collected for the commune and is not the house's money at any point.

This is what was charged, not what was collected — an unpaid balance still counts here and is chased on Money ahead. And nothing on this page knows what a room costs: heating a suite in February and the restoration still owed on it are real and are not in any column, so "earns most" is not the same as "makes most".

"Full" is nights sold against every night in the window, so a room listed halfway through the period reads low for a reason that is not its fault.
