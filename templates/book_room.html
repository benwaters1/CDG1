Worth Knowing

What You Are Booking Into

This is a restoration, not a renovation, and it has been running since 2013. The bed, the linen, the bathroom and the food are new and expensive. The walls around them are not, and will not be.

Under Class I listing — the same protection as Versailles — the plaster stays as the centuries left it. That is not neglect and it is not a style. It is the law, and it is the reason the room looks the way it does.
