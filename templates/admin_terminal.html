Which readers work

Stripe Reader S700 / S710 Works. Connects over Wi-Fi, so the till can drive it directly. The S710 also has 4G.
Verifone V660p, UX700, P630 Works, where available.
BBPOS WisePad 3, Stripe Reader M2 Won't work here. Bluetooth readers need a phone app holding the connection — Stripe has no way to reach them from a server.
Tap to Pay on a phone Won't work here either. It needs a native app approved by Apple. Usable through the Stripe Dashboard app, but the payment won't attach itself to a tab.

A payment taken on the reader needs the internet at that moment — there's no offline mode when the till drives the reader. If the Wi-Fi is unreliable where you serve, an S710 on 4G avoids the problem.
