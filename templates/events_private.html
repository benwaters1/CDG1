Un exemple An example

A Sample Private Retreat

Three nights from the retreats’ own days, shaped around your party. Every stay is drawn up with you.

  1. I

    Arrival

    • Welcomed with a glass of crémant, and shown to your rooms
    • The afternoon to settle in, and to wander the château and its grounds
    • L’apéritif in the Salon de Musique, then a candlelit welcome dinner
  2. II

    The Kitchen and the Market

    • A morning at the stove with the château’s chefs
    • Mirepoix on market day, beneath its medieval arcades, then the brocantes
    • A tasting of the region’s wines, then dinner by candlelight
  3. III

    The Restoration and Foix

    • A morning beside the restorers, as the painted beams return to the light
    • The castle at Foix, and lunch in the old town below it
    • The farewell banquet in the Salon de Musique, then dancing
  4. IV

    Au Revoir

    • A last, unhurried breakfast
    • A picnic bag each, packed by the kitchen, for the journey onward

If There Is a Reason

The château is yours for the length of your stay, and no other party shares its roof.

  • Milestone birthdays

    A fortieth, a fiftieth, a seventieth: long dinners under the chandeliers and the whole château to yourselves.

  • Anniversaries

    Return to the vows you made, with the people who were there.

  • Family gatherings

    Three generations under one roof, with nobody cooking but us.

  • Company retreats

    A leadership team, a board, a small team away: quiet salons to think in and a long table to talk at.

  • A party of friends

    Friends, a few nights, and nothing to organise.

La table The table

Every Meal, Cooked Here

At the Château’s Tables

Breakfast, long lunches on the terrace, dinners by candlelight in the salons: every meal is cooked and served by us, at the château’s own tables.

Menus follow the season and the markets of the valley, cooked in the château’s kitchens or on the grill in the grounds, and shaped with you before you arrive, down to the birthday cake. The bar is open to you and your guests throughout your stay, with the wines and sparkling of the south of France and choices without alcohol at every meal.

Les détails The details

The Finer Details

Included

  • Exclusive use of the château, its gardens and grounds, from three nights
  • One party at a time, with nobody else under the roof
  • Fifteen guests staying in the château’s bedrooms and suites
  • Planning with the château team before you arrive
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner, cooked and served by us
  • Wines, crémant and no-alcohol choices with every meal, and the bar
  • Flowers from the markets and the gardens, arranged by us
  • A historic tour of the château
  • The tea and coffee salon, the seasonal pool, tennis and pickleball, and Wi-Fi

Also possible, on request

  • Transport between Toulouse and the château
  • Musicians: string quartets, pianists and harpists
  • Further nights before or after your stay
  • Rooms for more guests at the places nearby

What Are You Marking?

One party at a time. Tell us the occasion, your dates and how many of you there are, and you will receive a written quote and our terms.
