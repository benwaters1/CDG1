Une fête privée A private celebration

Private Events

Smaller Than a Wedding, Longer Than a Dinner

The whole château for a few days: a birthday, an anniversary or a gathering of your own, with up to fifteen guests staying.

Throughout the seasons, the château welcomes private celebrations, bringing loved ones together within its historic salons, gardens and terraces. Each salon has its own stories to share, and aperitifs, dinners and music move from room to room by candlelight, exactly as the Marquis intended.

Entre soi Among your own

Private Hire

A Few Days of Your Own

For the birthdays and anniversaries that deserve more than a restaurant: up to fifteen of the people you choose, under one roof, and the evenings as long as you like.

Long lunches on the terrace, dinners by candlelight in the salons, mornings in the gardens and evenings that end when you decide. One party at a time, and the château is yours.

Les occasions The occasions

Whatever You Are Celebrating

The château is yours for the length of your stay, and no other party shares its roof.

  • Milestone birthdays

    A fortieth, a fiftieth, a seventieth: long dinners under the chandeliers and the whole house to yourselves.

  • Anniversaries

    Return to the vows you made, with the people who were there.

  • Family gatherings

    Three generations under one roof, with nobody cooking but us.

  • Company retreats

    A leadership team, a board, a small team away: quiet salons to think in and a long table to talk at.

  • House parties

    Friends, a few nights, and nothing to organise.

La table The table

Every Meal, Cooked Here

At the Château’s Tables

Breakfast, long lunches on the terrace, dinners by candlelight in the salons: every meal is cooked and served by us, at the château’s own tables.

Menus follow the season and the markets of the valley, cooked at the château’s tables or on the grill in the grounds, and shaped with you before you arrive, down to the birthday cake. The bar is open to you and your guests throughout your stay, with the wines and sparkling of the south of France and choices without alcohol at every meal.

{% endcall %}

Vos journées Your days

A Sample Stay

Three nights, shaped entirely around your wishes. This is a sample only.

  1. I

    The Day of Arrival

    • Welcome to the château with a glass of crémant, and the presentation of your guest chambers
    • Aperitifs in the gilded Salon de Musique
    • A candlelit welcome dinner in the Salle à Manger
  2. II

    The Celebration

    • A slow breakfast in the Salon de Porcelaine
    • A morning at the markets of the valley, or at leisure in the gardens
    • A historic tour of the château’s salons
    • Your celebration dinner by candlelight, with the cake and the music of your choosing
  3. III

    The Day After

    • A late breakfast, and a long lunch on the terrace or from the grill in the grounds
    • An afternoon by the pool or on the tennis court, in season
    • A farewell dinner under the chandeliers
  4. IV

    Au Revoir

    • A light breakfast before your departure
    • A little picnic bag each for your onward travels

What We Look After

Les détails The details

The Finer Details

Included

  • Exclusive use of the château, its gardens and grounds, from three nights
  • One party at a time, with nobody else under the roof
  • Fifteen guests staying in the château’s bedrooms and suites
  • Planning with the château team before you arrive
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner, cooked and served by us
  • Wines, crémant and no-alcohol choices with every meal, and the bar
  • Flowers from the markets and the gardens, arranged by us
  • A historic tour of the château
  • The tea and coffee salon, the seasonal pool, tennis and pickleball, and wi-fi

Also possible, on request

  • Transport between Toulouse and the château
  • Musicians: string quartets, pianists and harpists
  • Further nights before or after your stay
  • Rooms for more guests at the places nearby

Tell Us

What Are You Marking?

One party at a time. Tell us the occasion, your dates and how many of you there are, and you will receive a written quote and our terms.

A deposit of half the total, paid by bank transfer, reserves your dates; the balance is due on a date agreed before your arrival. All amounts paid are non-refundable, so we kindly ask that you arrange travel insurance.

Send an enquiry

If your party would rather spend its days learning something, ask us about the retreats: three to seven nights of la vie de château, with the kitchen, the brocantes and the château around you.
