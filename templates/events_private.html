The Shape Of It

Smaller Than a Wedding, Longer Than a Dinner

Most private hires run two or three days rather than one evening. The house is yours throughout — the salons, the kitchen, the gardens, the pool below the cherry trees.

Dinner is cooked in the Renaissance kitchen by Tom, our chef de cuisine, and served in the dining salon. Menus are built with you rather than chosen from a card.

Worth Knowing

A Working Restoration

The château has been under restoration since 2013 and is not finished. Ninety-four rooms; five are ready. The rest are in every state between ruin and repair.

Guests walk past that work rather than around it. It is the reason the house is worth taking, and the reason it costs what it does — the finished rooms pay for the rest.
