Money ahead Financials

Cash that is in the account and is not income. Until the guest stays, the dinner is served or the atelier runs, it is money owed back if the thing does not happen. It sits in the same balance as revenue and looks exactly like it.

This is not the same money as Money ahead

Money ahead counts these euros as incoming, which for cash flow is exactly right — they have arrived and they are yours to spend. This page asks the other question about the same euros: how much of the balance has actually been earned. Do not add the two together.
