The rota has never been compared with what is actually happening. A day can have guests in the house, a table booked and an atelier running, and nobody rostered at all — and nothing says so, because the two live in different tables. This asks the blunt question only: is there anybody. Whether the number on is enough is a judgement about the day, and not one this app should be making.
