What the house can manage

Rooms

There is no rating here and there is not going to be one. This is a thirteenth-century house: a room is not “accessible” or not, and a particular person either can or cannot manage a particular staircase. What this page does is put the facts about a room next to what a guest has told us in their own words, so somebody can pick up the telephone before the day they arrive rather than at the door.
