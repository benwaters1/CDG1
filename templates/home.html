L’art de vivre The art of living

La Vie de Château

For two centuries Gudanes was a château of guests. Then, for decades, it fell silent. Now, for a few weeks each year, its doors open again to a small company who come to live France as the French have always lived it.

Mornings begin with bread from the village boulangerie and crêpes on the terrace. The days belong to the kitchen and the château’s chefs, to the markets and brocantes of the Ariège, to the castle at Foix, or to the restorers’ scaffold as a fresco returns to the light. Afternoons are for the pool, a glass of the region’s wine and a book from the library. Every evening ends à table: the apéritif as the mountains turn blue, dinner of several courses by candlelight, and on the last night a banquet in the Salon de Musique, then dancing.

Guests arrive as strangers and leave as friends, and so many of them return.
