The Gudanes Experience

La Vie de Château

There is a particular pleasure in waking in a house like this: light across old stone, the valley below, and breakfast waiting. The days are slow here by design, with the pool, the grounds and the mountains on every side.

Nothing is staged. The house is lived in while it is brought back, and you are welcome in the middle of it: a guest, not a visitor, for as long as you stay.
