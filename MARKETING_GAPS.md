# What is still missing, ranked by what it would earn

Audited every public page against what a buyer actually needs: price signal,
social proof, scarcity, urgency, FAQ, testimonial, trust, email capture.

## Fixed this session

**No email capture anywhere.** The newsletter form existed but lived inside
the slide-out menu panel, which almost nobody opens. A reader who has just
finished a page about a fifteen-year restoration is the most likely
subscriber on the site. Now on nine pages, above the close.

**No trust signals on the booking page.** The one page where a card is
charged said nothing about who takes the payment, what happens if the château
declines, or how to change a booking. All six lines match what `book_room()`
actually does.

**No price signal on Events.** Your highest-value enquiry, and a couple could
not tell whether it was within reach. Now: the six things that shape a quote,
closing with "you will have a figure rather than a brochure."

## Still missing — in order of what I would do

### 1. Photographs of the rooms · high value, only you can do it
Not one of the five rooms has its own photograph; all use château interiors.
Someone deciding at €450 a night cannot see the bed they are buying. No copy
fixes this. **A phone and an hour of good light would do it.**

### 2. The translation switcher · currently damaging
`/language/fr` and `/language/es` are live. 10,700 words of prose are
untranslated, so a French visitor gets a French menu around an English page.
See TRANSLATION_GAP.md. Taking the switcher down is one line.

### 3. Guest testimonials on the pages that sell
Booking.com scores are on Stay, but Workshops, Events and La Table have no
quoted guest at all. You have the reviews; they are simply not placed. The
four workshop reviews currently shown are from nightly guests, not atelier
participants — worth correcting.

### 4. Scarcity, stated honestly
Seven bedrooms, one sitting a night, fifteen on an atelier, one event at a
time. All true, all genuinely limiting, and mostly unstated. This is the most
under-used asset on the site: the constraint IS the product.

### 5. A "press" page
The masthead wall on Restoration is strong. Vogue, AD Italia, House & Garden
and the Polignac medal deserve a page of their own with the actual coverage,
which also gives journalists somewhere to link.

### 6. Seasonal urgency
The mountain is open December to April. The gardens crop in summer. Brocante
season runs through summer. Nothing on the site says "this window closes",
because nothing is dated. A dated What's On would fix it.

### 7. Gift vouchers
An atelier or a night as a present. No mechanism exists, and it is a
straightforward addition to the booking flow — worth it before December.
